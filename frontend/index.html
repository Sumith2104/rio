Empirical 4-Algorithm Benchmark on Untouched Holdout Cohort (N = 885)
Research Finding & Selection Abstract:
"We trained and benchmarked 4 different Machine Learning algorithms: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, CatBoost, and XGBoost. We selected Logistic Regression / CatBoost because it gave the best balance: 86.10% accuracy and 81.69% recall on dropouts with a 0.9133 ROC-AUC score. We adjusted the decision threshold to τ = 0.42 so that we catch at-risk students 4.2 weeks before midterms."
